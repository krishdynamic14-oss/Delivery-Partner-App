# Google Play Data Safety Draft

Use this as a starting point in Play Console. Confirm final answers with the business owner before submission.

## Data Collection

The app collects data.

## Data Sharing

Data is not sold. Data may be processed by service providers used for app operations, including hosting/storage, push notifications, and communication/calling services.

## Security Practices

- Data is encrypted in transit where supported by the connected services.
- Users can request data/access removal through Dynamic Bazar admin/support.
- The app is intended for authorized company users.

## Data Types

### Personal Info

- Phone number
- Name, where configured for delivery partner/admin records

Purpose:

- Account management
- App functionality
- Company operations

### Location

- Approximate location
- Precise location

Purpose:

- App functionality
- Field operations tracking while app is open

### Photos and Videos

- Delivery proof photos
- Failed delivery proof photos
- COD/payment proof photos
- Stock proof photos

Purpose:

- Delivery verification
- Payment/settlement verification
- Inventory proof

### App Activity

- App interactions related to order updates, sync status, settlement submissions, and operational logs

Purpose:

- App functionality
- Operational audit

### Device or Other IDs

- Push notification token

Purpose:

- Send order assignment, deadline, settlement, and operational notifications

## Permissions To Explain

- Camera: capture delivery, failed delivery, payment, and stock proof photos.
- Notifications: send order assignment and operational reminders.
- Location: share live field location with admins while the app is open.

## Important Play Console Notes

- Mark the app as business/internal operations, not a public shopping app.
- If the app is only for Dynamic Bazar staff/partners, use closed/internal testing first.
- Ensure the privacy policy URL is public before production or wider testing.
