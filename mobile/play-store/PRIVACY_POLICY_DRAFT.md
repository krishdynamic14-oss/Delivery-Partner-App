# Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: 27 May 2026

Dynamic Bazar Delivery is an internal operations app for authorized Dynamic Bazar delivery partners and admins.

## Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information:

- Login mobile number and role details
- Assigned delivery order details
- Delivery status updates
- Delivery proof photos
- Failed delivery/RTO proof photos
- COD settlement details and proof images
- Daily stock proof photos
- Device push notification token
- Approximate and precise location while the app is open and the user is logged in
- App usage timestamps related to order sync and operational actions

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Assign and manage delivery orders
- Verify order delivery and failed delivery attempts
- Track COD collections and settlements
- Monitor stock availability and stock proof submissions
- Send order and deadline notifications
- Support admin monitoring of active field operations
- Improve operational accountability and customer delivery service

## Location Data

The app may collect location data only while the app is open and the user is logged in. Location is used for field route visibility and operational coordination by Dynamic Bazar admins.

## Photos and Files

Photos captured in the app are used as delivery proof, failed delivery proof, payment proof, or stock proof. These files are uploaded to Dynamic Bazar's configured company storage and linked to operational records.

## Data Sharing

Data is used for Dynamic Bazar internal operations. We do not sell user data. Data may be stored in company-managed systems such as Google Sheets, Google Drive, Firebase/Expo notification services, and configured communication providers used for app operations.

## Data Retention

Operational data is retained as needed for business records, settlement checks, delivery verification, and dispute handling. Admins may remove or archive data according to company policy.

## Security

The app uses role-based access and authenticated requests for app operations. Users should not share login credentials or devices used for company operations.

## User Choices

Users can contact Dynamic Bazar admin/support to correct account details, request access removal, or ask questions about operational records.
