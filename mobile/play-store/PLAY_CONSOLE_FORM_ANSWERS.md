## Store Listing

App name:
Dynamic Bazar Delivery

Short description:
Delivery, COD, stock proof, and route operations app for Dynamic Bazar partners.

Full description:
Dynamic Bazar Delivery is an operations app for authorized Dynamic Bazar delivery partners and admins.

Delivery partners can view assigned orders, plan delivery dates, call customers through the configured calling flow, confirm deliveries with OTP and proof photos, report refused or cancelled deliveries, submit COD settlements, capture daily stock proof, and sync field updates with the company system.

Admins can monitor orders, review partner activity, approve COD settlements, assign orders, check submitted stock proof photos, and view partner location updates where enabled.

This app is intended only for Dynamic Bazar's internal delivery operations and authorized users.

## Data Safety Summary

Data collection:
Yes

Data sharing/selling:
Data is not sold. Data may be processed by service providers used for app operations, including Google services, Expo/Firebase notifications, Google Apps Script/Sheets/Drive, AiSensy, and calling/communication providers configured by Dynamic Bazar.

Security practices:
- Data is encrypted in transit where supported by the connected services.
- Users can request account/data access removal through Dynamic Bazar admin/support.
- The app is intended for authorized company users.

Data types:
- Personal info: name and phone number for login, partner/admin records, and operational identity.
- Location: approximate and precise location while the app is open for field route visibility and proof coordination.
- Photos/files: delivery proof, failed delivery proof, COD/payment proof, and stock proof photos.
- App activity: order updates, sync status, settlement submissions, and operational logs.
- Device or other IDs: push notification token for operational notifications.
