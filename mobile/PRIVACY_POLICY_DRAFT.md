# Dynamic Bazar Delivery Privacy Policy Draft

Last updated: 18 May 2026

Dynamic Bazar Delivery is an internal operations app for Dynamic Bazar admins and delivery partners. The app is used for order delivery, delivery proof, COD settlement, stock updates, and operational notifications.

## Information We Collect

We collect partner/admin name, phone number, role, district, order assignment details, delivery status updates, COD payment details, UPI/payment references, delivery proof photos, payment proof screenshots, and call recording attachments for cancelled parcel proof. We also collect push notification tokens, device platform, device name, and app version to send operational alerts.

Order records may include customer name, phone number, WhatsApp number, address, district, area, pin code, product, quantity, order amount, payment mode, delivery OTP status, and delivery logs.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate admins and partners, show assigned orders, confirm delivery, record failed/cancelled delivery proof, manage COD settlement, update stock, send delivery and admin alerts, generate bills where configured, and maintain audit logs.

## Storage and Processors

Data may be stored in Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Apps Script, Firebase/Expo push notification services, and configured WhatsApp messaging services. These services are used only for app functionality and operations.

## Sharing

We do not sell user data. Data is shared only with systems required to run Dynamic Bazar delivery operations, such as Google services, Expo/Firebase push notification services, and the configured WhatsApp provider.

## Data Retention

Operational records are retained as required for order tracking, payment reconciliation, audit, and customer support. Local queued proof files are kept on the device only until sync completes or the pending action is removed.

## Security

The app uses secure local storage for session data, HTTPS network calls, scoped admin/partner access checks, and private project credentials. Users should keep their phone and login password secure.

## Contact

For privacy or data questions, contact Dynamic Bazar support/admin using the official business support channel.
